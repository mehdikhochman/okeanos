### Voice and mood

- Short, lowercase product names: "cluster earrings", "organic rings", "new in".
- Summer, social and playful: 🪭 🥂 🌤️ 🍒 🌺 ✨ 🌿, with "Match with your friend!" and
  "Your favorite hand chain is back!"
- Recurring phrases: "Available now", "New in", "Dm for orders".
- Reels use Afro-house and Arabic pop: *Yamore* (MoBlack, Salif Keïta, Cesária
  Évora) and *Tamally Maak* (Amr Diab).

### Photography style (from the 36 photos)

The feed uses two consistent kinds of shot:

1. **Flat-lays on white crumpled linen.** A single piece, centred, in soft daylight.
   The white background makes these easy to cut out for the site.
2. **Golden-hour shots on the Lebanese coast.** Models in white linen or pastel
   pink tie-dye, with pebble beaches, limestone rocks, the sea and warm sun on
   skin. Props: a folding fan, white sunglasses. Typical framing is a hand at the
   neck or cheek, so earrings, rings and bracelets all show in one shot.

The **design** is chunky, sculptural, "molten" gold: puffy drops, wavy hoops,
wrap rings, leaf and flower shapes, mixed with fine chains and pastel accents
(mint enamel, beads).

So the brand has two sides: the **wordmark image is cool, minimal and gallery-like**,
and the **feed is warm white linen with golden sunlight**. The site uses the
grey plaster and navy as the frame and the gold pieces and sunlit photos as the
warmth (see `FRONTEND_PLAN.md`).

### Wordmark (from `assets/brand/instagram-wordmark-reference.jpg`)

- **Typeface:** a high-contrast Didone serif in all caps (Didot or Bodoni
  style) with wide letter spacing. Ask the client for the original font or an SVG.
- **Ink:** deep navy **`#032537`** on a cool-grey plaster wall, **`#D7D7D7`**.
- **Art direction:** a single vertical white impasto brushstroke with visible bristle
  ridges, trailing off into dry-brush texture at the bottom. The wordmark sits
  across it.

## 3. The brand's own guide (Instagram "Guide" highlight)

The client shared screenshots of the four-slide Guide highlight, posted about 119
weeks before 2026-10-17. The slides are navy or light grey, with the text in
capitals in a serif face, matching the site palette. The text below is the
substance of each slide, in sentence case:

1. **Title:** "Everything you need to know about Okeanós Jewels"
2. **What do you need to know about Okeanós jewelry?** "Our pieces are designed
   and created by us, and all our products are stainless steel & gold plated."
3. **How can my jewelry last longer?**
   - Don't wear it 24/7, because constant wear reduces the life of the pieces.
   - Apply products like perfume and lotion before putting it on.
   - Remove jewellery before showering, swimming, sports and sleep, so it stays
     "as the day you received it".
4. **Can I return or exchange?**
   - No: "you cannot return or exchange your orders as every piece is made upon request."
   - If the size doesn't fit, "you can give it back to us and we will fix the size for free."

The prototype uses all of this: the Guide section, the product accordions, the
announcement strip and the "Made to order, free resizing" note.

### okeanosjewellery.co is probably a different business

That site uses the same spelling, "Ōkeanós", but its policies contradict the
guide. It promises waterproof pieces and a 30-day replacement guarantee. The
Instagram guide says to take pieces off before showering and swimming, and that
nothing can be returned or exchanged. **Do not use anything from that site.**
Ask the client once to rule it out.

## 5. Photography direction

- **Keep both existing shot types.** They're consistent and on brand.
  - White-linen flat-lays → product pages and cut-outs
  - Golden-hour coast shots → hero moments, the About page, the lookbook strip
- **For the interactive wall:** a transparent PNG or WebP cut-out of every piece.
  The linen flat-lays mask easily. A new set shot on grey plaster (`#D7D7D7`),
  lit from the upper left, would match the wordmark image exactly.
- At least 3 images per piece: flat-lay, on-body and a macro detail.
